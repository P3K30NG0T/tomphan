What is an H100 really worth?

A GPU cloud buys an H100 for tens of thousands of dollars and rents it by the hour. Whether that earns its cost of capital depends on three things investors argue about: rental prices, how busy the GPU stays, and how many years it stays useful. CoreWeave depreciates GPUs over six years. Critics such as Michael Burry argue the economic life is two to three.

Method

How the model works

  1. Revenue per year = price × 8,760 hours × utilization. Price falls by the decline rate each year.
  2. Costs = power (kW × hours × utilization × $/kWh) plus other opex per available hour.
  3. Depreciation runs straight-line over the economic life. Tax is 21% of EBIT; a loss creates a tax shield used by the wider company.
  4. Free cash flow = EBITDA − tax, plus resale value in the final year. NPV discounts at the WACC; IRR is solved by bisection.
  5. The JavaScript, a separate Python script and the Excel file all give the same base-case NPV.

    Paid channels without the 30% toll

    Written for a hypothetical Southeast Asian messaging app with 70 million monthly users. Telegram figures come from public reporting and are graded by reliability below. As of 7 Oct 2026.

    To
    Head of Strategy
    From
    Tom Phan, Strategy Analyst
    Date
    7 October 2026
    Re
    Launching paid channels: which payment route?

    1. Recommendation

    Launch paid channels and paid posts in two phases, and route as many payments as possible outside app stores.

    • Phase 1 (months 0–6): Android and web first, paid through local e-wallets and bank QR at about 1.5%. On iOS, offer in-app purchase so the feature works everywhere.
    • Phase 2: an in-app credit that creators can spend on promoting their channels, so fewer credits are cashed out after an app-store fee.
    • Not now: crypto rails. Keep them optional where they are clearly legal.

    At the base assumptions, fans would spend about $3.15M a month. A mixed route costs 10% in payment fees instead of 30%, leaving about $0.63M more each month for creators and the platform.

    2. What Telegram shows

    • Payers are few. Premium reached 15 million subscribers, only about 1–1.5% of a billion users. Plan for low conversion and high value per payer.
    • Telegram did not avoid app stores. Stars are bought through in-app purchase and Apple and Google keep 30%. Telegram lowered the effective cost by subsidizing ads bought with Stars, and by letting crypto users top up through Fragment.
    • Hype fades. Mini App reach peaked at 1.44 billion in September 2024 and settled at 150–190 million by mid-2025. Useful apps stayed; tap-to-earn games did not.

    3. The economics

    Change the inputs to see how the payment route splits each fan's dollar. The share of payments that start on iOS is the input to replace first with the app's own data.

    Fan spending / month–
    Payment fees–
    Platform revenue / year–

    Where each dollar goes

    Your mix compared with routing every payment through app stores, and with avoiding them entirely.

    4. App-store rules

    • In most markets, Apple and Google require their own billing for digital goods sold inside an app.
    • In the US, a court barred Apple in April 2025 from charging commission on purchases made through a link to the web. The appeals court has since sent the question of a fair commission back to the trial court, and Apple has taken the case to the Supreme Court. This is unsettled and US-only; do not plan on it for Southeast Asia.
    • The larger lever in our markets is where a payment starts: Android and web sales avoid most of the fee.

    5. Risks

    • Creator supply: paid content needs creators with loyal audiences. Start with verified creators in education, market news and entertainment.
    • Store policy: a payment link that breaks store rules can get the app pulled. Each market needs legal review before launch.
    • Fraud and refunds: wallet payments need refund rules, and creator payouts need identity checks.
    • Moderation: content behind a paywall is harder to review.

    6. Next 90 days

    1. Pull the real split of paying actions by operating system from app analytics.
    2. Pilot with 200 creators in one market. Track conversion of followers to payers, revenue per payer and refund rate.
    3. Negotiate e-wallet rates, targeting 1.5% or less.
    4. Go to Phase 2 if at least 1% of a channel's followers pay and refunds stay under 2%. These thresholds are starting points to test.
